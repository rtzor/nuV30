## Minimal candidate and clock contract

Add `V30_MUXED_YIELD`, requiring all of `V30_BIU_YIELD`, `V30_BUS_OWNERSHIP` and `V30_MUXED_AD`. Reject each missing prerequisite explicitly even if BIU_YIELD itself is absent. Existing BIU_YIELD plus MUXED_AD without the new authorization must retain its named rejection and all prior gates. Default ports and behavior remain unchanged.

The candidate only authorizes this configuration and adds a combinational core pad permission `!BUS_OWNED` under the new macro, with common low/high drive-enable terms used identically in the two actual AD tri-state assignments and AD_OE. Preserve raw BIU ad_o/ad_oe, last_ad_hi/lo, t1_half2, scheduling, architectural state and SS addresses/version. No new synthesized flop, opcode rule or exception. Existing BIU yield inhibits new work and conserves EU requests/debts. Inverted controller CORE_BUS_ENABLE, including acquisition, supplies BUS_OWNED; actual EXCLUDE/YIELD_READY remain arbitration inputs. External synchronous isolation of all other controls/transactions remains required; the combinational mask is not a glitch-free physical output-enable or native pad-timing guarantee.

Core CE and CE_HALF remain live during ownership. C-a forbids coincident enables; S-1 requires at least one HALF between CEs. In this first multiplexed contract the frontend/controller CE is the same actual core CE, and REQUEST/YIELD_PENDING are registered on that CE; no unrelated fabric-rate controller, direct mid-half pending changes or asynchronous pins are covered. Remote pulses may begin around HALF but remain valid through a core CE and resolve synchronously. Do not disable or alter existing bus_half equivalence/reconstruction assertions to accommodate a failure.

Any mechanism change beyond the candidate requires a separately committed preregistration before replacement acceptance. Retain failures; no relaxed numeric bar or fitted opcode timing.
